05

Voice & tone.

How we sound

  • Lead with the result, not the process.
  • Short, declarative sentences. Cut filler.
  • Back claims with numbers we can defend.
  • Premium, never boastful. Show, don't shout.
  • Warm and direct with clients; confident in public.

Words

Use: engineered, retention, authority, compounding, precision, reach, system.

Avoid: guru, ninja, hacks, “go viral guaranteed”, hype without proof, corporate filler (“leverage”, “synergy”).

Before → after

“We leverage cutting-edge synergies to help you go viral!”
“We engineer content that holds attention — and compounds.”

06

Logo & the E-mark.

E-mark — icon contexts only: favicon, avatars, app icon, watermark.

QuidEdge.
QuidEdge.

The wordmark is the logo. The E-mark is a supporting icon — never place it directly before the wordmark (it doubles the “E” and muddies the name).

Do

  • Give the wordmark clear space equal to the cap-height of “E”.
  • Use the E-mark solo for favicons, avatars, and watermarks.
  • White on dark, or ink on paper — always full contrast.

Don't

  • Don't prefix the wordmark with the E-mark.
  • Don't recolour outside ink, paper, or proof blue.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows.

08

Typography.

Manrope everywhere. The signature move is the weight anchor: a light headline with one word in heavy 800 to land the point — echoing the bold “E” in the logo.

We engineer viral impact.

Aa

Light 300 · body & big headings

Aa

Semibold 600 · labels & UI

Aa

Extra-bold 800 · anchor word & stats

Eyebrows/labels: 0.6rem, uppercase, letter-spacing ~0.22em, stone. Headlines: negative tracking (−0.03 to −0.05em). One anchor word per headline — no more.

09

Imagery & motion.

Photography

Cinematic and moody. Desaturate bright/busy backgrounds so subjects sit inside the dark palette (never a raw, clashing studio colour). Real people over stock. Deep shadows, controlled highlights.

Video & thumbnails

Retention-first pacing, kinetic captions, restrained motion graphics. Thumbnails: one clear subject, high contrast, one idea. Never cheap transitions that cheapen a premium brand.

Graphic devices

Hairline section rules, oversized “ghost” numbers, the scrolling proof marquee, and subtle film grain — used consistently to signal the same editorial system across pages.

Motion

Calm and intentional. Slow reveals, soft easing (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)), nothing bouncy. Motion should feel expensive, not busy. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

10

Applying the brand.

Social

E-mark avatar, ink background, proof-blue accents. Weight-anchor headlines on covers & thumbnails.

Decks & proposals

Manrope, ink slides, one blue accent per slide, defensible stats, wordmark on cover & footer.

Email & docs

Signature = wordmark + primary tagline. Minimal formatting. Warm, precise copy.

11

Governance.

Before it ships: does this look and sound like the same brand as our homepage?

  • Wordmark used correctly, full contrast, clear space respected.
  • ≤ 5% proof blue; the rest ink, paper, stone.
  • Manrope, one anchor word per headline.
  • Every claim/number defensible.
  • Voice: result-first, no hype, no filler words.
  • Imagery on-palette; motion calm.

Own this document. Update it as the brand evolves — but change it deliberately, together, never by accident. A brand is a promise kept consistently.
