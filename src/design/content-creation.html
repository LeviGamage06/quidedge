Before we start.

Is content creation only video editing?

No. The scope can combine copy, branded visuals, scripts and video content according to your audience and goals.

Is social media management included?

It can be discussed alongside production. The proposal defines whether publishing, account management and community responsibilities are included.

How do you price the work?

Pricing depends on formats, volume, production complexity and review requirements. QuidEdge primarily serves businesses planning to invest AUD 1,000+ per month; this is not a fixed package price.
