How progress is assessed

Measure what matters to your business.

Choose metrics connected to your goals: relevant audience reach, meaningful engagement, profile or website visits and qualified inquiries. Follower counts and views provide context; they do not independently prove business value.

Before you decide

Your questions, answered.

Which platforms do you support?

Discuss LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram management with the team. YouTube and TikTok content production can be included in a wider plan when appropriate.

Are all platforms included in one price?

No universal platform bundle is assumed. The proposal defines the channels, content volume, production needs and management responsibilities.

Do you guarantee followers or leads?

No. Results depend on the audience, offer, starting point, content and distribution. We agree on relevant measures and review performance.

Are paid ads included?

Paid advertising responsibilities, creative production and any media budget should be scoped explicitly. A social management plan does not automatically include ad spend.

Can I approve posts before publishing?

The approval workflow is agreed during onboarding, including who reviews content and how revisions are handled.
