Before you get in touch

Make a confident next move.

Can we work with QuidEdge remotely?

Yes. QuidEdge delivers its services online. We focus on businesses in Australia, the USA, New Zealand and Canada. Share your time zone and availability so collaboration and feedback can be planned around your team.

Which service should we start with?

Start with personal branding when the owner’s expertise needs a clearer voice; company branding when the business message or identity needs alignment; content creation when you need execution; and business development guidance when you need to evaluate your offer, audience or acquisition priorities.

What does AUD 1,000+ per month mean?

It is a fit guideline for ongoing support, not a fixed package price or a promise that every service is included. Your proposal defines the price, deliverables, platforms, production volume and responsibilities.

What will you need from our team?

Your goals, audience context, existing brand materials and relevant content are a useful starting point. Time for interviews, recording, feedback and approvals depends on the scope and is discussed before work begins.

How are feedback and approvals handled?

Review stages, the person responsible for approval and revision arrangements should be defined in the agreed scope. Share your internal approval requirements during the initial conversation.

How do we assess progress?

Choose measures relevant to the work: message consistency and audience response for branding; engagement and retention for content; and qualified inquiries or proposal outcomes where suitable data is available. Views and followers alone do not establish sales impact.
