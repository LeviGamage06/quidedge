Before you decide

Your questions, answered.

What is included in company branding?

The scope can combine positioning, messaging, visual direction, content pillars and social profile alignment. Specific deliverables are agreed in your proposal.

Is this only a logo project?

No. The focus is the relationship between your audience, offer, message and presentation. Visual identity is one part of that system.

Can you manage content after the branding work?

Yes. Content creation and social media management can be discussed as a follow-on engagement, with platforms, volume and responsibilities agreed separately.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the research, strategy, identity and execution you need. We define the scope and quote before work begins.

Will branding guarantee more sales?

No. Branding can improve how clearly you communicate your value. Sales also depend on demand, your offer, distribution, pricing and the customer experience.
